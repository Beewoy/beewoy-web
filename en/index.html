FAQ

Straight answers.

What does the monthly price include?
Website design and launch, hosting, SSL and technical care. The Start plan also includes maintenance, backups, on-page SEO and routine content updates as agreed.
What isn't included?
Your domain, paid third-party services, extensive copywriting and features outside the plan. The plan also doesn't cover a Google Business Profile, Search Console or ad campaigns. We quote anything extra up front.
How quickly will my website be ready?
We launch a simple website within 48 hours of approving the brief, provided your texts and photos are ready. For larger websites, we agree on a timeline based on scope.
Can I change the website later?
Yes. On the Start plan, we make routine content updates for you as agreed. On the Basic plan, a small change costs from €15; we quote larger changes up front.
What if I cancel?
You can cancel at any time, with no commitment. We host and run the website, so we switch it off when the plan ends.
I already have a website. Can you redo it?
Yes. We'll take a look and tell you honestly whether it's worth improving or rebuilding from scratch.
