FAQ

Terms, plainly.

Is there a minimum term?
No. You can cancel at any time. We host and maintain the website, so we switch it off when the plan ends.
When do I start paying?
Only once your website is live. There is no setup fee.
What isn't included?
Your domain, paid third-party services, extensive copywriting and features outside the plan. Google Business Profile, Search Console, backlinks and campaigns are handled separately. You always approve the price up front.
How long does delivery take?
We launch a simple website within 48 hours of approving the brief, if your content is ready. For larger websites, the timeline depends on scope.
